Expanded InfoCard — 3 variations on B (direction tabs + ranked list)

Same symbol, buildHierarchy. All three: direction chosen first, kinds with zero connections omitted, ranked by count, one category selected at a time, external/unresolved shown as a muted footer. They differ in how much detail a row carries and how selection and reset are shown. Interactive.

B1 · Tabs, bar rows, status strip

Closest to B. Bars sit under each label and span the full row. Selecting a row shows a strip stating what the graph is doing, with a Clear link.
